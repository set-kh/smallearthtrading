導入事例Case Studies

ケース１: 自社製品をカンボジア向けに輸出Case 1: Exporting original products to Cambodia

お客様の課題:Client's Challenge: テストマーケティングのために自社製品をカンボジアへ輸出したいが、手続き方法がわからず不安。 Wanted to export their own products to Cambodia for test marketing but was unsure and anxious about the procedures.

提案と結果:Our Solution: 弊社が書類作成から現地配送までを一貫してサポート。初めての輸出でも安心して進められるよう、煩雑な手続きをすべて代行し、ご希望通りのスケジュールで納品準備を整えました。 We provided end-to-end support from documentation to local delivery. We handled all complex procedures so they could proceed with confidence even on their first export, and prepared the delivery according to their desired schedule.

ケース２: 店舗用の物資をまとめて輸送Case 2: Bulk transportation of store supplies

お客様の課題:Client's Challenge: 海外店舗で使用する備品や資材をまとめて送りたいが、輸送コストを抑えたい。 Wanted to send fixtures and materials for their overseas store in bulk but needed to keep transportation costs down.

提案と結果:Our Solution: 弊社では重量ではなく「体積」に基づいた見積もりを採用しており、密度の高い商品（液体類、金属加工品など）を送るのに最適です。お客様には段ボールにお好きな物を詰めていただき、効率的かつ経済的に輸送ができる方法をご案内しました。「自由度が高く、コストパフォーマンスも良い」とご好評をいただいています。 We use quotes based on "volume" rather than weight, which is ideal for sending high-density items (liquids, processed metal goods, etc.). We advised the customer that they could pack whatever they liked into cardboard boxes, and guided them on an efficient and economical method of transport. We have received positive feedback for the "high degree of freedom and excellent cost performance."

ケース３: レストランのこだわりのお酒を日本から買い付けCase 3: Sourcing a restaurant's signature drinks from Japan

お客様の課題:Client's Challenge: カンボジアでレストランを経営されているお客様。日本でしか手に入らないこだわりの日本酒・焼酎をお店に置きたいが、日本の酒販店から直接カンボジアへ送ってもらうことができず、買い方も輸送方法もわからない。 A customer running a restaurant in Cambodia wanted to stock carefully selected sake and shochu available only in Japan, but Japanese liquor shops would not ship to Cambodia directly, and they did not know how to buy or transport them.

提案と結果:Our Solution: 買い付け代行をご利用いただきました。公式LINEで銘柄と本数をお知らせいただき、弊社が日本国内の取扱店を探して買い付け・受け取り・割れ物梱包のうえ、通関から店舗までの配送まで一貫して対応。お客様はお店にいながら、お好きな銘柄を定期的に仕入れられるようになりました。酒類は銘柄によってお取り扱い条件が異なるため、事前のご相談のうえで進めています。 They used our Purchasing Agent Service. The customer simply sent us the brands and quantities via LINE; we located a Japanese retailer, purchased and received the bottles, packed them as fragile goods, and handled everything from customs clearance to delivery to the restaurant. The customer can now restock their favourite brands regularly without leaving their shop. As handling conditions for alcohol vary by product, each request is confirmed with us in advance.

ケース４: 欲しい商品の写真を送るだけ、複数店舗の買い回りをおまかせCase 4: Just send a photo — we shop across multiple stores for you

お客様の課題:Client's Challenge: 欲しい商品が複数のお店に分かれており、1件ずつ購入手続きをして日本の住所で受け取る手段がない。都度カンボジアへ送ると送料がかさんでしまう。 The items they wanted were spread across several different stores, and they had no way to place each order and receive it at a Japanese address. Shipping each item to Cambodia separately would have made the shipping costs add up.

提案と結果:Our Solution: 欲しい商品が見つかり次第、写真や商品ページをLINEで送っていただき、弊社が順次買い付け・受け取り。商品がすべて揃った時点で1つにまとめて梱包・発送することで、輸送代金を抑えつつ、お客様は「写真を送るだけ」で完結します。 Whenever the customer found something they wanted, they sent us a photo or product page via LINE, and we purchased and received each item in turn. Once everything had arrived, we packed and shipped it all together, keeping the shipping cost down — all the customer had to do was send photos.

Customers inform us of the luggage information such as packing style and weight in advance, and send it to our warehouse in Japan by mail or courier. We handle all customs procedures and local delivery as a one-stop service.

Q. 日本の商品を代わりに買って送ってもらえますか？Q. Can you buy products in Japan on my behalf and ship them to me?
